About IndustrialSkills

Learn From Someone Who Actually Runs the Plant

Most exam prep content is written by people who've never stepped inside a power station. IndustrialSkills is different.

Who's Behind IndustrialSkills

I'm a working Assistant Engineer at a 500 MW thermal power station in India, with over 10 years of hands-on experience across three major plant systems:

  • Coal Handling Plant (CHP) — wagon tippling, crushers, conveyors, stacker-reclaimers
  • Ash Handling Plant (AHP) — bottom ash and fly ash systems, ESP operations, ash disposal
  • Generation Systems — boiler operations, turbine systems, control room operations

Over the years, I've mentored ITI trainees, diploma holders and engineering graduates preparing for NTPC, state genco AE/JE, BOE and MPPGCL recruitment exams — and noticed the same gap every time: textbooks explain the theory, but almost nobody explains how these systems actually run on-site, under real operating conditions.

Why IndustrialSkills Exists

IndustrialSkills exists to close that gap — turning years of real plant experience into exam-ready study material, written in plain language, focused on exactly what recruiters and interview panels actually ask.

  • No copy-pasted textbook definitions — explanations grounded in real operating experience
  • Content mapped directly to power sector recruitment exams (NTPC, genco AE/JE, BOE, MPPGCL)
  • A mix of free articles, quizzes and affordable eBooks so quality prep is accessible to everyone

What You'll Find Here

  • Free articles on plant systems and exam strategy
  • Free mock tests with instant scoring
  • Affordable eBooks covering full plant operations and India's power sector
